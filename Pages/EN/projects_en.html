Diary

A free online agenda project, simple, intuitive and using as little personal data as possible (only the user's email and name). You can create an account, log in, create your events and view them on your personal space. The project is made with the Next.js framework and the Neon database.

Ambitious and original project for a site to order custom chocolate strawberry desserts. You can create an account, log in, create your chocolate strawberry, validate your order and download the invoice in PDF from your personal space. Other features are planned such as payment via PayPal.

Real functional e-commerce site for natural hygiene and beauty products. You can create an account, log in, choose products from the selection and validate your order, then your order will be delivered to your home. The site is made via the Odoo e-commerce and shop management platform.
